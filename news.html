Talk 🇨🇳ShenzhenChina

跨文化设计与 AI 交互实践 — Cross-cultural Design & AI Interaction

Chaihuo Tech Night Talk · 柴火创客技术碰撞夜

At Chaihuo Maker Community, I shared a design journey across China, Italy, Switzerland, and Japan — how cultural context shapes problem framing, and why design is cultural expression, not only technology.

Through AI × hardware × interaction work — interactive installations, wearables, and digital products — we walked from concept to system design and prototyping, and discussed moving from tech-driven to experience-driven outcomes and from demos into real use.
